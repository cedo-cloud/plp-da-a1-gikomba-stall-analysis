## Business Problem

Wanjiku operates a fresh-produce stall at Gikomba Market
and needs to understand her sales performance, identify
growth trends, determine which products require restocking,
and decide where to focus her business efforts. This
analysis uses two weeks of daily sales and current stock
records to generate practical recommendations using
core Python.

## Top Three Findings

1. Total sales were KES 46,450, with sales increasing
   by 10.66% in Week 2.
2. Average weekend sales were KES 4,900, which was
   82.50% higher than the weekday average.
3. Tomatoes and Sukuma were below their reorder levels,
   with gross margins of 33.33% and 40.00%, respectively.
